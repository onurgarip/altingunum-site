Privacy Policy

Effective date: 16 November 2026

In short: your groups, members, amounts and payments stay on your device. No account, no ads, no tracking. If you turn on iCloud, data is stored in your own iCloud account, not with us. We collect only anonymous usage data to improve the app, and you can turn that off in Settings with one tap.

1. Data controller

Altın Günüm is developed by Onur Garip, an individual developer, who is the data controller under Turkey's Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK) and the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).
Contact: onur@ogio-entertainment.site

2. Data that stays on your device

The gold days you create, member names and phone numbers, amounts, draw results, payment records and notes are stored only on your device. We cannot access them and they are never sent to our servers.

3. iCloud sync and sharing (optional)

When iCloud sync is on, your groups are saved through Apple's CloudKit service to the private database in your own iCloud account and synced across your devices. This data lives on Apple's infrastructure, tied to your Apple Account; we cannot see it.

When you share a group, people you send the iCloud share link to can view that group read-only (group name, members, order, amounts and payment status). You decide who to share with and can stop sharing at any time. iCloud data is governed by Apple's privacy policy.

4. Contacts access

If you allow it, you can pick people from your contacts when adding members. Only the name and phone number of the contacts you select are imported and stored on your device. Your address book is never sent to us. You can revoke access at any time in iOS Settings.

5. Face ID / app lock

If you enable the app lock, authentication is handled by iOS. Your face or fingerprint data stays in Apple's secure hardware; neither the app nor we can access it.

6. Notifications

Reminders are local notifications created on your device. We don't use a remote (push) notification server.

7. WhatsApp and SMS reminders

When you choose to remind a member, the app opens WhatsApp or Messages with a pre-filled message. You are the one who sends it, and sending is subject to that app's own terms. We don't see the message or the recipient.

8. Gold and currency prices

Prices are fetched directly from your device from public sources (TCMB, Truncgil, GenelPara, Gold API). As with any internet connection, those servers see only your device's IP address and standard connection details. No group or personal information is included in these requests.

9. Anonymous usage data (analytics)

To understand which parts of the app are used and to fix problems, we collect anonymous product usage data via PostHog (US cloud). This includes events such as "group created" or "draw made", plus technical details like app version, iOS version, device model and language.

  • Never sent: names, amounts, phone numbers, contacts or group contents.
  • A randomly generated anonymous ID is used; it does not identify you and is not linked with other apps.
  • We don't use the advertising identifier (IDFA), don't track you across apps and don't record your screen.
  • On by default; turn it off any time under Settings › Anonymous usage data.

Legal basis: our legitimate interest in improving and safely operating the app (KVKK Art. 5/2-f, GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)). Because this data is processed on servers in the United States, it is transferred abroad with the safeguards required by applicable law.

10. What we don't do

  • We don't require an account.
  • We don't show ads, and we don't sell or rent your data.
  • We don't take payments; there are no money transfers in the app.

11. Retention

Data on your device and in iCloud stays until you delete it. Anonymous usage data is kept for at most 12 months, then deleted or aggregated.

12. Your rights and deleting your data

Under KVKK Art. 11 and the GDPR you have the right to access your data, ask for correction or deletion, object to processing and lodge a complaint. In practice:

  • In-app data: delete groups one by one, remove everything with Settings › Delete all data, or delete the app to erase all data on the device.
  • iCloud data: iOS Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Manage Storage › Altın Günüm. Stop sharing a group from within the app.
  • Anonymous usage data: turn it off in Settings, then email onur@ogio-entertainment.site to have it deleted; we'll help you identify the anonymous ID.

We respond within 30 days. You may also complain to the Personal Data Protection Authority in Turkey or your local data protection authority in the EU.

13. Children

The app is intended for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

14. Changes

If we update this policy, we'll publish the new version on this page with its effective date and announce significant changes in the app.

The Turkish version of this policy prevails in case of any discrepancy.
