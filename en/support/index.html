Frequently asked questions

Do I need to set up a gold day before trying the app?

No. Tap "Örnek günü gör" (See a sample day) on the home screen to explore a ready-made sample group. The sample never affects notifications or widgets, and you can remove it any time.

How do I share a group with friends?
  1. Make sure you're signed in to iCloud on your iPhone and iCloud sync is on in the app's Settings.
  2. Open the group and tap Share.
  3. Send the link via WhatsApp, Messages or any way you like.
  4. Your friend opens the link and sees the group read-only in their own Altın Günüm app, and can pick their own name from the list with "Who am I?".

Only you can edit the group; your changes reach everyone you shared it with automatically. To stop sharing, choose "Stop sharing" from the same screen.

A shared group doesn't show up for me.

The device opening the link needs to be signed in to iCloud and have Altın Günüm installed. Check your internet connection and reopen the app. If it still doesn't appear, write to us.

Where is my data stored?

All your groups are stored on your device. If you turn on iCloud sync, they're also backed up to your own iCloud account. No account is needed and we can't see your data. See the Privacy Policy for details.

How do I delete my data?
  • One group: open the group and choose Delete from its menu.
  • Everything: in the app, Settings › Delete all data. Deleting the app also removes all data on the device.
  • iCloud data: iOS Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Manage Storage › Altın Günüm.
  • Anonymous usage data: turn off Settings › Anonymous usage data in the app, then email us to delete anything already sent.
How current are gold and currency prices?

Prices come from public sources such as TCMB, Truncgil, GenelPara and Gold API and refresh when you open the app. There can be small differences or delays between sources.

Prices are for information only and are not investment advice. Check your jeweller's or bank's current price before buying or selling.

I'm not getting reminder notifications.

Check that notifications are allowed under iOS Settings › Notifications › Altın Günüm. You can also adjust reminder times in the app's Settings. Focus modes may delay notifications.

How do WhatsApp reminders work?

When you want to nudge a member who hasn't paid yet, the app opens WhatsApp with a friendly pre-filled message. You can edit it and send it yourself; the app never sends anything on your behalf.

Does the app transfer money or gold?

No. Altın Günüm is only a record-keeping and tracking tool. Payments happen between members, outside the app.

Is the app paid? Are there ads?

Altın Günüm is free, with no ads and no in-app purchases.
